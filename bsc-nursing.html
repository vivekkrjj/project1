About the B.Sc Nursing Programme

B.Sc. Nursing is a four-year undergraduate nursing degree that combines classroom learning, laboratory skill development, clinical experience, community health and professional nursing practice. At Bhumi Nursing College, students can use classroom study, practical learning and supervised clinical exposure to build professional nursing knowledge and patient-care skills.

Eligibility & Qualification

10+2 Science (PCB) + English; INC eligibility includes 45% aggregate in PCB. Minimum age 17 years on or before 31 December of the admission year; medical fitness required.

Important: Eligibility, admission dates, seats, counselling and other conditions can change according to the current competent-authority notification. Students should confirm the applicable rules for their admission session.

What You Will Learn

📚 Theory & Nursing Sciences

  • Nursing foundations and professional practice
  • Anatomy, physiology, nutrition and health sciences
  • Pharmacology and safe medication practices
  • Community and public-health nursing concepts

🩺 Practical & Clinical Skills

  • Basic patient assessment and nursing procedures
  • Infection prevention and patient safety
  • Communication, documentation and care planning
  • Supervised practical / clinical learning as applicable

Career Opportunities

  • Other nursing roles subject to registration and recruitment rules

🎓 Further Education

  • Professional skill development and continuing education
  • Future specialization pathways according to applicable regulations
