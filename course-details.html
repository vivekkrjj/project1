🎓 B.Sc Nursing

A professional undergraduate nursing programme covering nursing theory, practical skills, community health and supervised clinical training.

Duration4 Years
LevelUndergraduate
Entry10+2 / equivalent
ModeRegular

Eligibility / Qualification

  • 10+2 passed with Science subjects: Physics, Chemistry and Biology (PCB).
  • English is a required subject.
  • INC eligibility criteria specify 45% aggregate in PCB for the qualifying examination.
  • Candidate should meet the applicable medical-fitness and admission requirements.

What you study

  • Fundamentals of Nursing and Nursing Foundation
  • Medical-Surgical Nursing
  • Community Health Nursing
  • Child Health / Pediatric Nursing
  • Maternal & Obstetric Nursing
  • Mental Health Nursing
  • Nutrition, Pharmacology and related nursing sciences

Career options

  • Staff Nurse / Registered Nurse after applicable registration
  • Hospital and clinical nursing services
  • Community and public-health services
  • Further study such as Post Basic B.Sc Nursing / M.Sc Nursing subject to eligibility

Admission selection

  • Admission may involve merit and/or an entrance process prescribed by the competent authority.
  • Students should follow the current state/university/college admission notification.
  • Seat availability, counselling and fee rules are subject to the applicable admission authority.

⚕ GNM — General Nursing & Midwifery

A professional nursing and midwifery programme focused on patient care, community health, clinical practice and nursing responsibilities.

Duration3 Years
LevelDiploma
Entry10+2 / equivalent
SciencePreferable

Eligibility / Qualification

  • 10+2 with English from a recognized board and minimum 40% marks as per INC criteria.
  • Science is preferable.
  • Recognized State Open School / NIOS candidates may be eligible subject to the applicable rules.
  • Registered ANM candidates with pass marks are also covered by INC eligibility provisions.
  • Candidate should be medically fit and satisfy the current admission authority requirements.

What you study

  • Nursing Foundation
  • Medical-Surgical Nursing
  • Community Health Nursing
  • Child Health Nursing
  • Midwifery and obstetrical nursing
  • Mental Health Nursing
  • Nutrition, first aid and practical clinical care

Career options

  • Hospital / clinical nursing roles
  • Community health and primary-care services
  • Maternal and child health services
  • Further nursing education subject to eligibility and current regulations

Admission notes

  • Admission is generally conducted once in an academic year.
  • Selection, counselling and fee structure follow the applicable state/university/college rules.
  • Always check the current admission notice before applying.

📜 ANM — Auxiliary Nurse Midwifery

A foundational nursing and midwifery programme preparing students for community-based healthcare, maternal-child care and basic nursing services.

Duration2 Years
LevelDiploma
Entry10+2 / equivalent
FocusCommunity & Maternal Care

Eligibility / Qualification

  • 10+2 from a recognized board in the streams permitted under the applicable INC criteria.
  • Arts / Science / Health Care Science vocational pathways may be eligible as prescribed.
  • English requirements apply according to the applicable eligibility route.
  • Candidate should be medically fit and meet the current admission authority requirements.

What you study

  • Community Health Nursing
  • Primary healthcare and health promotion
  • Maternal health and midwifery
  • Child health and nutrition
  • Basic nursing care and first aid
  • Health education and community field work

Career options

  • Community and primary healthcare services
  • Maternal and child health support
  • Health sub-centres and related healthcare settings, subject to recruitment rules
  • Further nursing education where eligible

Admission notes

  • Admission is generally once in an academic year.
  • Selection, counselling, seats and fees depend on the applicable authority.
  • Check the latest college/state notification before submitting documents.

📂 Documents Usually Required

Other supporting documents

Important: Do not submit original certificates unless the admission authority specifically requires them for verification. The final checklist should be confirmed from the current admission notice of the college / university / state authority.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply for these nursing courses?

Students who meet the course-specific educational, age/eligibility, medical-fitness and other conditions prescribed by the applicable nursing and admission authority can apply.

Is Science compulsory for every course?

No. B.Sc Nursing requires the prescribed Science (PCB) route. GNM has Science as preferable, while ANM has additional eligible educational pathways under the applicable INC criteria.

What documents should I bring?

Keep academic certificates, identity proof, photographs and applicable domicile/category/medical/transfer documents ready. See the Documents section above.

Can I apply online?

Use the college's current admission instructions. Where online registration is enabled, complete the application and keep the submitted form/receipt for your records.

What happens after submitting an application?

The college/admission authority may verify eligibility and documents, then proceed with merit, counselling or entrance-based selection as applicable.

Are the fees and admission dates fixed?

No. Fees, seats, counselling and admission dates can change by academic session and authority. Students should rely on the latest official college/admission notice.
